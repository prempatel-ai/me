From Rank 5764 to Rank 366 on the Lunar Lander Leaderboard

Nov 9, 2026 · 3 min read

In my last post I wrote down a plan for climbing the Lunar Lander leaderboard. I went back to check, and my rank moved. A lot.

My first upload was at rank 5764, with a result of 242.71. Now the same leaderboard shows my agent at rank 366.

5764 → 366my rank on the Lunar Lander leaderboard

The numbers

Here they are side by side.

Rank
5764 before, 366 now. That’s a jump of 5398 places.
Result
242.71 before, 275.31 now.
Mean reward
258.99 before, 294.11 now.
Std reward
16.28 before, 18.80 now.

What actually improved

The result is the mean minus the std, so I looked at which part moved. The mean went up by about 35 points, from 258.99 to 294.11. The std went up a little too, from 16.28 to 18.80.

So I only really improved half of the score. My agent lands better on average, but it isn’t more consistent than before. If the std had stayed where it was, the result would have been a few points higher.

A better average and a steadier agent are two different goals.

Which agent this is

The agent behind this score is the PPO model from my hands-on post, trained on 16 copies of LunarLander-v3. The same score shows up twice in the table, once for lunar_lander_v3 and once for LunarLander-v3.

How far there still is to go

The agent that was at rank 5 when I last looked had a result of 361.31, from a mean of 370.25 and a std of only 8.94. I’m at 275.31, so there’s still a big gap. To close it I need both things, a higher mean and a lower std.

What I’m going to do next

I don’t want to keep changing settings on the same agent and hoping the number moves. I want to learn more reinforcement learning first, and then come back with better tools. The course goes on to Q-Learning next, and I’m curious what other techniques I can bring back to this leaderboard.

It’s the exploration and exploitation tradeoff again. I could keep polishing this one agent, which is exploitation, or go learn new things and come back, which is exploration. For now I’m choosing to explore. Then I’ll be back to climb higher.

Prem signing off. ✍️
